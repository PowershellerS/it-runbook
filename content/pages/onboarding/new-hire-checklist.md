Run through this checklist as soon as HR confirms a start date — aim to finish hardware and accounts at least 2 business days before day one.

## Before day one

- Create accounts: email, SSO/identity provider, and any team-specific tools from the role's access template.
- Order and image a laptop; apply the standard security baseline and MDM enrollment.
- Provision a phone extension or softphone license if the role needs one.
- Add the new hire to the relevant distribution lists and calendars.
- Prepare a welcome email with login instructions and who to contact for help.

## Day one

- Hand off hardware (or confirm shipping tracking for remote hires).
- Walk through first login, MFA enrollment, and password manager setup.
- Confirm access to the team's shared drives and core apps.
- Point them to this Runbook.

## First week

- Follow up on any access requests that needed manager approval.
- Check that VPN and any restricted systems are working from their actual work location.

## Offboarding, for reference

When someone leaves, reverse this list same-day: disable accounts, revoke MFA devices, wipe and recover hardware, and remove from distribution lists and shared drives.
